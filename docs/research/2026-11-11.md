## 3. Techniques for open issues

- **#334** (restart adopts no pods on stormpump): upstream's model is that the runtime owns sandboxes and the kubelet re-lists them (containerd's restart test asserts sandboxes/containers survive and are re-listed); dockershim persisted sandbox checkpoints on disk. Our `list_pod_sandbox` is in memory; the same two options apply: persist a checkpoint (pod uid, sandbox, container handles) under the state root and re-adopt from it, or have the engine list its workloads. [containerd restart_test.go](https://cos.googlesource.com/third_party/containerd/+/refs/heads/release-R129/integration/restart_test.go). Prior-art reading was shallow (search only).
